HOW TO RUN

WASD · Mouse
Move, look
Space
Jump (once more in the air). Jump at a glowing wall to run along it, jump again to kick off
C / Ctrl
Slide (while running): under pipes, and it keeps your speed
Shift
Dash where you're going, once per jump
Left click
Shoot (6 shots; blade kills give 2 back)
Right click
Blade: kills up close, and swung as a shot arrives it sends the shot back
Q (hold)
Focus: time slows; kills refill it
R
Back to the last checkpoint
Esc
Pause

CONTROLLER

Works with Xbox, PlayStation (DualShock 4 / DualSense), Steam Deck and Steam Controller pads. The button names change to match the pad you're holding.

TOUCH SCREEN

Left thumb anywhere on the left: move. Drag on the right: look (you can drag across FIRE too). Buttons: JUMP, SLIDE (hold), DASH, FIRE (hold), BLADE, FOCUS (hold). Turn your phone sideways.

One hit and you're down, but you're straight back at the last checkpoint. Theirs is one hit too.
